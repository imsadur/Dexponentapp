# Landingv3 design system

Landingv3 is a separate editorial product story. It uses warm off-white content fields, deep navy typography, cobalt actions, acid-lime proof points, and dark market-data sections. Typography uses Outfit with wide editorial headings.

## Structure

- Floating public navigation.
- Artistic asymmetric hero with a maximum 920px headline width and a live product preview.
- Dense opportunity, workflow, and network sections.
- Clear split between liquidity-provider and Farm-manager journeys.
- High-contrast final action and structured footer.

## Components

- Large headline typography with no more than three lines.
- Rectangular buttons with 10px radii and strong text contrast.
- Farm lists use editorial rows and one featured market panel instead of repeated generic cards.
- Motion uses restrained stacking and pinned storytelling on large screens, with reduced-motion support.
