# UX principles

Research synthesis, 7 September 2026. The PDF is the product specification; the supplied JSX is a concept reference.

1. Begin with strategy intent, then a template. Preserve the same configuration structure across strategy families.
2. Explain where capital goes with a live flow and allocation breakdown.
3. Use one primary action at each stage. Keep expert controls in labeled disclosures.
4. Separate strategy validation from deployment readiness. A valid configuration does not imply audited contracts or an executable transaction.
5. Preserve work automatically, expose save failures, and resume at the last valid stage.
6. Present fees, provenance, and risks beside performance. Never infer safety from APY.
7. Display liquidation and margin risk alongside leverage; do not fabricate exchange liquidation quotes.
8. Use explicit demo and hypothetical labels. A preview success never becomes an ACTIVE onchain farm.
9. Use native accessible inputs, visible focus, keyboard-operable cards, and focus-contained dialogs.
10. Keep desktop density deliberate; collapse the summary and navigation on mobile.

Visual decisions: Inter, charcoal-green surfaces, mint primary action, muted borders, moderate radii, amber caution. Retain the wireframe's calm tone and immediate preview while adopting the PDF's typography and six steps.
